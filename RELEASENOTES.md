## Version 1.0.3

* Added - ChasePlane 2024 support. (Please enable ChasePlane Settings in POPM preferences to allow usage of ChasePlane 2024 camera views)
* Added - Option to close Pop Out Panel Manager after successfully executing pop out process.
* Fixed - Application crash when executable file was renamed.
* Fixed - Auto pop out process incorrectly initiated multiple times during start of flight session or not initiated at all after clicking ready to fly button.
* Fixed - When hide title bar option is enabled, adjusted size and location of pop out to correct coordinates and size based on pop out window shadow and border width. Previously, the size and cooridnate were off by few pixels.
* Updated - When hide title bar option is enabled, using keyboard shortcuts to move pop out window is disabled.
* Updated - SU5 support.
